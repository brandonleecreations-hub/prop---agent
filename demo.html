This demo works exactly like the real game: every pack holds 3 foil cards, duplicates don't stack. Higher tier cards hide in packs too — the higher the tier, the tougher the pull (a Legend is about 1 slot in 50). In the real game, pulling a card you do not own yet UNLOCKS it on your tier list — ready to battle in Grid Clash; pulling one you already own saves a foil copy instead. THE RING — the signed, one-of-a-kind chase card — hides in about 1 pack in 200. The Demo Chase Pull button guarantees a Ring pull in this demo so you can see the celebration — in the real game there is no chase pack: THE RING hides in regular packs.
