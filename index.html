Build Your Ticket

1 · Tap a sport — its props pop up right away  ·  2 · Future Games — props lock when the game tips off  ·  3 · Tap MORE or LESS on a card — it's on your slip. Pick at least 2 to lock a ticket.

Optional: add a fully custom leg

Copy each line exactly as your betting site shows it — your player, your line, your More / Less pick, and which site it came from (PrizePicks, Underdog, DraftKings, FanDuel, Sleeper…). Prop Agent tracks live stats against your line where ESPN reports the stat. Tennis stats like Aces, Double Faults and First Serve % aren't in the ESPN feed, so those legs get a tap-to-track stepper on your ticket.
